Your answers, on one screen

Your calendar knows who invited you, but it makes you open every event to see what you answered — and it forgets what an event looked like before somebody changed it. RSVP Buddy reads your calendar and puts the answers on one screen.

Three tabs, three questions

Agenda

What's on?

Everything in your calendar, day by day, with your reply shown on every invitation. Scroll back to see what has already happened, or jump to any date.

Going

What did I commit to?

Invitations you accepted and events you added yourself, for the next 12 months. The last thing you said yes to is the first thing you see.

Invitations

Who is still waiting for me?

To answer: no reply or only a Maybe. Declined: the ones you declined. Past: the ones that went by without a yes.

It remembers what your calendar forgets

  • When you accepted — “Accepted 12 Nov”, right on the event.
  • What changed — rescheduled, moved to another place or renamed, with the old value next to the new one.
  • What disappeared — a cancelled or deleted event stays in the list, crossed out, instead of silently vanishing.

RSVP Buddy works this out by comparing your calendar with what it saw the last time you opened the app, so the history starts on the day you install it. Tap any event to see the time, the place, the notes, who else is invited and what they answered, and everything that changed.

Private by design

  • Read-only. RSVP Buddy can't change, create or delete anything in your calendar.
  • No account, no sign-up, no tracking.
  • Nothing leaves your iPhone. There is no server.

RSVP Buddy shows the calendars set up on your iPhone — iCloud, Google, Exchange and others — the same ones you see in the Calendar app. To answer an invitation, use Calendar as usual; RSVP Buddy shows the result.
