RSVP Buddy — Know what you said yes to.

RSVP Buddy is a free, read-only iPhone companion to the Calendar app. Your calendar knows who invited you, but it makes you open every event to see what you answered — and it forgets what an event looked like before somebody changed it. RSVP Buddy reads your calendar and puts the answers on one screen.

Three tabs answer three questions. Agenda: what's on? Everything in your calendar, day by day, with your reply on every invitation. Going: what did I commit to? Accepted invitations and your own events for the next 12 months. Invitations: who is still waiting for me? No reply or only a Maybe, declined, and the ones that went by without a yes.

It remembers what the calendar forgets: when you accepted, what changed (rescheduled, moved or renamed, with the old value next to the new one) and what disappeared (a cancelled or deleted event stays in the list, crossed out). The history starts on the day you install it.

Private by design: read-only, no account, no sign-up, no tracking, no server. Nothing leaves your iPhone. Made for busy, forgetful people — parents most of all.
