RSVP Buddy — Support

RSVP Buddy shows which calendar events you have accepted, which invitations are waiting for your answer, and how your events have changed.

Common questions

The Invitations tab is empty.

It lists only events that someone else invited you to and that you have not accepted. Events you created yourself never appear there, so it is empty when nobody is waiting for your answer.

An accepted event says "Accepted" without a date.

Your calendar does not record when you accepted. RSVP Buddy notes the date itself, so it only has it for invitations you accepted after installing the app.

How do I answer an invitation?

In the Calendar app. RSVP Buddy is read-only; it shows your reply as soon as you come back.

I don't see my events.

Check that the calendar is visible in the Calendar app, and that RSVP Buddy has full access in Settings → Privacy & Security → Calendars.

What does a badge mean?

Tap the info button at the top right of any tab: the guide explains every tab and every badge.
