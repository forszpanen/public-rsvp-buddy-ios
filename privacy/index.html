RSVP Buddy — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

RSVP Buddy is an iPhone app that shows which calendar events you have accepted, which invitations are waiting for your answer, and how your events have changed.

The short version

RSVP Buddy does not collect, send or share any of your data. Everything stays on your iPhone.

What the app reads

With your permission, RSVP Buddy reads the calendars set up on your iPhone: event titles, times, places, notes, links, the people invited and their replies. It needs full calendar access because that is the only kind that includes your reply to an invitation.

RSVP Buddy only reads. It cannot create, change or delete events, and it cannot answer invitations.

What the app stores

To show when you accepted an invitation and how an event changed, RSVP Buddy keeps a history of the events it has seen. This history is stored only on your iPhone, in the app's own storage. It is not uploaded anywhere. If you back up your iPhone, the history is part of your own backup, like other app data.

Deleting the app deletes this history.

What the app does not do

  • No account and no sign-in.
  • No server: the app makes no network connections of its own.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking.
  • No data is shared with or sold to anyone.

Tapping "Open in Maps" or a link inside an event opens Apple Maps or your browser; what happens there is covered by their own privacy policies.

Your choices

You can take calendar access away at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Calendars. The app then shows nothing until you turn it back on.

Children

RSVP Buddy collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.
